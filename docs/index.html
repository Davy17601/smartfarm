SwiftUI · MVVM · CoreData · Xcode 13 · iOS 14 · Swift 5.0 · no third-party deps · Khmer UI
A finance + activity + scheduling app for Cambodian small-scale farmers. Four tabs, each owned by a teammate and built via a /skill command from its docs/*.md spec. Each page renders the expected result of one screen; Process Flow shows how data moves through the app. The app has 4 tabs — Dashboard · Finance · Calendar · Settings — with Reports & Backup reached from inside Settings.
